## Full description

The call ends. A commitment begins.

Recall helps you remember the follow-ups, meetings and small promises that keep work moving. Speak naturally after a call, review the details and give your next step a reminder.

SPEAK IT BEFORE YOU FORGET
“I spoke with Arjun. We’ll discuss the PPT tomorrow at 6 PM. Remind me one hour before.”

Use your phone’s speech recognition to capture your words in English. Check the name, action and timing before saving. A person’s name is optional.

FOLLOW THROUGH YOUR WAY
• Add a follow-up, reminder or meeting.
• Type an action manually whenever you prefer.
• See today’s commitments and upcoming tasks.
• Mark actions complete and find your past conversations.
• Keep each person’s interaction history together.
• Set a specific reminder time and optional advance alert.

REMINDERS ON YOUR PHONE
With notifications and alarm permissions enabled, the Android app schedules reminders on your device. Already scheduled alerts can work without internet. Saving and syncing records require connectivity. Delivery can be affected by phone permissions, battery restrictions and force-stopping the app.

FREE TO USE
No subscription. No payment required. Built to help salespeople, founders, leaders and anyone who wants to follow through on their commitments.

ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY
A Recall account is required to save and sync your records. Review speech recognition results before saving. You can request account deletion in Settings or through our website.
